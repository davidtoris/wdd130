About Us - Exciting Expeditions

We are a family owned and operated business that has been providing whitewater rafting adventures for over 20 years. We are passionate about the outdoors and love sharing our love of whitewater rafting with others.

History

The company was founded in 1996 by two friends who loved whitewater rafting and wanted to share their passion with others. They started with a small fleet of rafts and a few guides, and have since grown to become one of the most respected whitewater rafting companies in the world.
